About the Company

Built around the part of the trade that usually goes wrong.

Rionex Exim Private Limited is an international trading company dealing in ferrous and non-ferrous metal scrap, incorporated in Maharashtra in 2025 and operating from its facility at Pelhar, Vasai.

We trade in both directions — importing copper, aluminium and brass scrap from suppliers in the United Arab Emirates, Georgia and Germany for Indian rod mills, smelters and foundries, and exporting to processors abroad. In a trade where most disputes come down to loose grading, skipped inspections and paperwork that will not clear customs, we work to three fixed rules.

Specified, not described

ISRI codes on every contract. No lot is offered as "mixed copper" or "good quality scrap".

Inspected at origin

Third-party PSIC before loading, with container-wise photographs and seal numbers.

Priced on the exchange

LME cash settlement as the reference, with the discount stated in writing.

What We Trade

Copper, aluminium and brass — by grade.

All material is traded against International Scrap Recycling Industries specifications and valued on London Metal Exchange benchmarks. Grades outside this list can be sourced on enquiry.

Copper

Cu · LME Grade A

The core of our book. Copper scrap moves to rod mills, wire manufacturers and secondary smelters that need consistent, specification-compliant feed — where grade accuracy directly affects recovery and yield.

ISRI · Barley

Bare Bright Copper Wire

Clean, untinned, uncoated, unalloyed wire, 16 gauge and heavier. The premium grade, minimum 99.9% copper.

ISRI · Berry

No. 1 Copper Wire

Clean, unalloyed, uncoated wire and cable, free of insulation, burnt wire and brittle material.

ISRI · Candy

No. 1 Copper Solids

Clean copper pipe, tube, bus bar and solids, free of plating, coatings and non-metallic attachments.

ISRI · Birch / Cliff

No. 2 Copper

Unalloyed copper wire, tube and solids with minor contamination. Minimum 96% copper recovery.

ISRI · Druid

Insulated Copper Wire

Copper wire with plastic or rubber insulation, traded on assessed recovery percentage.

Refined

Copper Cathodes

Electrolytic cathode, 99.99% purity, LME registered brands. Supplied against confirmed demand.

ISRI · Clove / Cobra

Copper Wire Granules

Granulated copper from cable processing, graded by purity and particle size.

ISRI · Pallu / Pale

Copper Tube Scrap

Clean pipe and tube offcuts from plumbing, refrigeration and HVAC applications.

ISRI · Telebirch

Tin-Coated Bus Bar & Cable

Tinned copper bus bar, transformer strip and enamelled winding wire.

Aluminium

ISRI · Troma

ISRI · Talon

ISRI · Tablet

ISRI · Telic

How a Shipment Runs

Nine steps from enquiry to settlement.

The sequence below is the one we follow on every contract. Nothing in it is skipped to close a deal faster — the steps exist because each one is where this trade commonly breaks.

Enquiry & Specification

Grade, quantity, destination port and delivery term confirmed in writing before any price is quoted.

Counterparty Verification

Incorporation documents, trade licence, export permissions and references checked and recorded.

Firm Offer

Full corporate offer issued, stating LME reference, quotational period, discount and validity.

Contract Execution

Sales and purchase agreement signed, setting Incoterms, tolerance, rejection terms and arbitration seat.

Payment Instrument

Irrevocable letter of credit established at sight, or an alternative agreed in the contract.

Pre-Shipment Inspection

Independent agency inspects, weighs and seals at the loading port. PSIC and radiation certificate issued.

Loading & Documentation

Container-wise photographs, seal numbers, bill of lading, packing list and certificate of origin prepared.

Customs Clearance

Documents filed for clearance at the discharge port, with the clearing agent briefed ahead of arrival.

Discharge & Settlement

Final weight and quality determined at discharge. Variance settled by debit or credit note.

Quality & Compliance

The standards we contract against.

Each of these appears in our contracts as a stated obligation, not as a marketing claim. Certificates are furnished with the shipping documents on every consignment.

Specification

ISRI Scrap Specifications

The international standard for scrap commodity classification. Every lot carries its ISRI code on the contract and invoice.

Valuation

LME Benchmark Pricing

Priced against published London Metal Exchange cash settlement figures for a stated quotational period.

Inspection

SGS · Bureau Veritas · Intertek

Third-party pre-shipment inspection at the loading port, with weight and quality certification.

Delivery

Incoterms® 2020

CIF, CFR and FOB terms, with risk transfer and cost allocation stated explicitly in the contract.

Safety

Radiation & Hazard Clearance

Certificate of non-radioactivity and non-explosive material issued at origin for every container.

Regulatory

Basel Convention Compliance

Material declared and documented in line with transboundary movement rules for recoverable metal.

Sourcing Markets

Where our material comes from.

We import from established supplier networks in three markets, each chosen for the grades it reliably produces and the documentation standards its exporters work to. Delivery terms and destination port are agreed contract by contract.

United Arab Emirates

Gulf Collection Hub

A consolidation point for scrap arriving from across the Gulf and East Africa, with short transit times to Indian west-coast ports and well-established free-zone export procedures.

Georgia

Caucasus Corridor

Access to material originating across the Caucasus and Central Asia, shipped through Black Sea ports. A growing source of copper and aluminium wire grades.

Germany

European Industrial Scrap

Industrial and post-consumer scrap from one of the most tightly regulated recycling markets in the world, with consistent grading and dependable documentation.
